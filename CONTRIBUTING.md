# Contributing to Guttr

Guttr is at the proposal and ideation stage. Feedback on design, methods, accessibility, and product scope is welcome.

## Ways to help

- Comment on an [open product decision](docs/open-decisions.md).
- Propose or critique a creation workflow.
- Suggest research methods or accessibility requirements.
- Identify license-safe sources or offer an original visual asset collection.
- Report unclear terminology or assumptions in the documentation.

## Before submitting code or artwork

A software license, contributor terms, and an artwork distribution policy have **not** been finalized. Please do not submit proprietary code, third-party graphics, or respondent data until explicit contribution and rights arrangements exist.

For now, proposals and discussions are the safest starting point.

## Proposal format

Describe the user need, suggested approach, possible alternatives, major risks, and how success could be evaluated. Distinguish observations from assumptions.
