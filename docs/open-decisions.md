# Eight open product decisions

These are deliberately unanswered questions for product and research collaborators.

1. **Survey parity and phasing:** Which Tally-like features are essential in the MVP, and which belong in later releases?
2. **Licensing and compensation:** Which asset licenses and artist payment or revenue-sharing models will the library support?
3. **Published asset versions:** If an artist updates an asset, should published surveys stay pinned to a historical version or opt into updates?
4. **Collaboration permissions:** Who can edit a project, review changes, publish, restore versions, and manage respondent data?
5. **Multimodal limits:** What restrictions should apply to video length, file size, storage duration, and participant uploads?
6. **Experiment Room editing:** Can imported paper artwork be edited at the layer level, or is correction handled through re-upload?
7. **Hosting:** Will launch support platform-hosted URLs only, or also embeds and custom domains?
8. **Research archive and exports:** Which responses, media, metadata, consent records, and survey snapshots must be preserved and exportable?

## Decision record template

For each decision, record: options considered; user impact; research impact; legal or technical dependencies; chosen approach; owner; decision date; and whether the decision is reversible.
